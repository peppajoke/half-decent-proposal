About the Tauren Incident

You rolled a Tauren in Classic. You got bored. You were right.

Classic by yourself is a forty minute walk across the Barrens to kill eleven zhevras for a man who wants twelve. That is not content. That is a chore with hooves. We're not going to pretend it was fun. We were there. We were also bored. We were just too stubborn to leave.

What's actually different in Forever

  • A reimagined Azeroth. Familiar roads, new places on them. Nine new dungeons from level 13 up, including an underwater city off Stranglethorn and a sewer route into Dalaran.
  • No realm roulette. You pick a ruleset instead of a realm: Normal, PvP, or Roleplay. Everyone on it is one big pool, so we are always on your server.
  • Two part names. First name and surname, unique as a pair. "Lauren" is probably taken. "Lauren Healsthefools" is not.
  • Campfires that matter. Sit at a camp for a minute, get buffs from whoever's there. The game now rewards the thing you'd do anyway.
  • Account wide collections and Legacy Points. Pets and progress follow you between characters, so a tamed zoo is never wasted.
  • Raids on December 9. Ten, twenty, and forty player versions. We will need a healer for all three sizes. We will need you for all three sizes.

And this time you have us.

That is not nothing. It is not a lot. Honestly it's three men and one of them is Phil. But it is not nothing.
